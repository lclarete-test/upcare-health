Our approach

Curiosity, supported by evidence.

UpCare Health publishes accessible stories about health, wellbeing, beauty and nutrition.

How we use sources

We draw on published research and guidance from public health agencies and professional medical organizations. Sources are linked within each story. We distinguish observational findings from experiments and acknowledge limitations.

What our stories provide

Our content is educational. It does not offer individual diagnoses or treatment plans. Articles are not represented as medically reviewed unless a qualified reviewer is specifically credited.

Corrections and updates

We aim to correct factual errors and update stories when evidence changes. Publication and revision dates are shown on article pages.
