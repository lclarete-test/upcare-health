Our standards

Our approach to evidence

Every UpCare story starts with an everyday question and follows the evidence as far as it can honestly go.

We begin with a clear question

We define the claim we want to examine before we write. That keeps a story focused and helps us separate a useful answer from an attractive headline.

We look for the strongest available sources

Our research prioritizes peer-reviewed journal articles, systematic reviews and guidance from public-health agencies and recognized medical organizations. When we discuss early laboratory or animal research, we say so clearly.

We show our work

Sources are numbered inside every article and linked in a dedicated Sources section. Readers can open the research, see where a claim came from and assess it for themselves.

We describe the limits

A single study rarely settles a health question. We distinguish association from cause, animal findings from human evidence and a population average from personal medical advice. We avoid turning uncertainty into certainty.

We review and update

Each story includes a publication date. We revise material when stronger evidence or authoritative guidance changes the answer.

UpCare Health provides general information, not diagnosis or individual treatment. Read more about our standards in our Editorial Policy.
